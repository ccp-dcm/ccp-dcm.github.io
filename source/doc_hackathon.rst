Documentation hackathon 14-16 December 2026
-------------------------------------------

STFC and the Firedrake team will host a documentation hackathon at Rutherford Appleton
Laboratory on 14-16 December 2026.

The aim of the workshop is to document anything and everything related to the Firedrake
project - you are allowed to work on anything providing that it isn't writing code
(excluding demos)! Examples of useful things to work on include (but are not limited to):

* Adding/improving docstrings in Firedrake, UFL, pyadjoint, etc
* Adding pages to the `Firedrake manual <https://www.firedrakeproject.org/documentation.html#manual>`__
* Adding new demos
* Adding type hints

Travel
------

The postcode for the Rutherford Appleton Laboratory is OX11 0QX.

The nearest train station is Didcot Parkway, and the
`Thames travel X24/X34/X35 buses <https://www.oxfordbus.co.uk/services/THTR/X24>`__
travel between Didcot Parkway Station and the Harwell Campus (~ 30 minute journey,
with a bus every ~ 15 minutes). Taxis are also available at Didcot Parkway Station.

Detailed travel instructions are provided on the STFC
`"How to Find Us" page <https://www.ukri.org/who-we-are/stfc/facilities/rutherford-appleton-laboratory/how-to-get-to-rutherford-appleton-laboratory/#contents-list>`__.
