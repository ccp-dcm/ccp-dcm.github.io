Documentation hackathon 14-16 December 2026
-------------------------------------------

STFC and the FEniCSx and Firedrake teams will host a documentation hackathon at Rutherford Appleton
Laboratory on 14-16 December 2026.

The aim of the workshop is to document anything and everything related to the FEniCSx and Firedrake
projects - you are allowed to work on anything providing that it isn't writing code!
(demos allowed) Examples of useful things to work on include:

* Adding/improving docstrings in dolfinx, Firedrake, UFL, pyadjoint, etc
* Adding pages to the `Firedrake manual <https://www.firedrakeproject.org/documentation.html#manual>`__
* Adding new demos
* Adding type hints

Where and when
--------------

The hackathon will be held 14-16 December at the Visitor Centre on STFC's Harwell Campus.

.. important::

   To be able to access the site you  must bring a valid ID (passport, eVISA or driving licence)

The hackathon is expected to be an unstructured event, but an approximate
programme for it is as follows. We will also try to arrange dinner plans for each evening.

Monday 14th
~~~~~~~~~~~

* 10:00 Arrive
* 10:00-11:00 Initial project ideas discussed and split into groups
* 11:00-12:00 Begin project work
* 12:00-13:00 Lunch
* 13:00-16:30 Project work
* 16:30-17:00 End of day catch up/report

Tuesday 15th
~~~~~~~~~~~~
* 09:00-12:00 Project work
* 12:00-13:00 Lunch
* 13:00-16:30 Project work
* 16:30-17:00 End of day catch up/report

Wednesday 16th
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~
* 09:00-12:00 Project work
* 12:00-13:00 Lunch
* 13:00-15:30 Project work
* 15:30-16:00 Final catch up/report
* 16:00 Leave

Travel
------

The postcode for the Rutherford Appleton Laboratory is OX11 0QX.

The nearest train station is Didcot Parkway, and the
`Thames travel X24/X34/X35 buses <https://www.oxfordbus.co.uk/services/THTR/X24>`__
travel between Didcot Parkway Station and the Harwell Campus (~ 30 minute journey,
with a bus every ~ 15 minutes). Taxis are also available at Didcot Parkway Station.

Detailed travel instructions are provided on the STFC
`"How to Find Us" page <https://www.ukri.org/who-we-are/stfc/facilities/rutherford-appleton-laboratory/how-to-get-to-rutherford-appleton-laboratory/#contents-list>`__.
